Researching the high-energy universe

I am a PhD student in astroparticle physics in the Astroparticle Physics Theory group at DESY, working with Dr. Andrew Taylor. I obtained my M.Sc. in astronomy from the School of Astronomy and Space Science at Nanjing University, where I conducted research at the Key Laboratory of Modern Astronomy and Astrophysics.

My research explores how cosmic rays propagate, interact, and radiate in the Milky Way and its surrounding halo. I develop numerical simulations of cosmic-ray diffusion, nuclear spallation, and radioactive decay, alongside theoretical models that connect interstellar magnetic turbulence to cosmic-ray transport. I also analyze observations of Galactic gamma-ray sources to investigate particle transport in their surroundings.
