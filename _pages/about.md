  <p>
    My research explores how cosmic rays propagate, interact, and
    radiate in the Milky Way and its surrounding halo. I develop
    numerical simulations of cosmic-ray diffusion, nuclear spallation,
    and radioactive decay, alongside theoretical models that connect
    interstellar magnetic turbulence to cosmic-ray transport.
    I also analyze observations of Galactic gamma-ray sources
    to investigate particle transport in their surroundings.
  </p>

## Current work

My current work focuses on cosmic-ray transport in the Milky Way's extended circumgalactic medium (CGM) halo. The diffusion model is presented in [Galactic Cosmic-Ray Transport in the Giant Circumgalactic Medium Halo](https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-4357/aeaba1), published in _The Astrophysical Journal_ (2026). I am extending my diffusion model to include advection, energy losses, and reacceleration. On the pulsar-halo side, I use the morphology of TeV halos such as Geminga and Monogem to diagnose the interstellar magnetic field ([PRD Letter](https://doi.org/10.1103/vt3s-rbj1), [ApJ 2026](https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-4357/ae5f67)). See the [publications](/publications/) and [research](/research/) pages for details.
